# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want this project to be a friendly and respectful place for everyone.

Please:

- Be respectful and kind to others.
- Use inclusive and appropriate language.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on helping the project and its community.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not allowed:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Any other inappropriate or disrespectful behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers.

We will review reports and take appropriate action when necessary.

Thank you for helping keep our community welcoming and respectful.
